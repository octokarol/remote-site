If you have any questions, feedback, or need help with Remote, feel free to reach out at karolpoczta@windowslive.com. We'll do our best to get back to you as soon as possible. It helps to mention your TV's brand and model.

Can't find your TV?

  • Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Local Network access for Remote in the Settings app under Privacy & Security.
  • Turn the TV on. You can also add it manually with its IP address, which you'll find in the TV's network settings.

Connecting for the first time

Most TVs show a prompt to allow the remote, or a code to type into the app. Remote's TV Guide, in the app's Settings, lists what each TV system supports.
